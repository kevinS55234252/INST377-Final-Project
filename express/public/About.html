We are fellow anime watchers who struggle to find good shows to watch at times. MyAnimeList makes it tedious to find anime that hits certain itches, and that’s where RANime comes in. You can find top animes, or even random ones, in certain genres, themes, and rating scores! If you are simply here to find the a random anime on MyAnimeList, you can simply glance at the home page and look through the options. However, if you are interested in finding a more specific anime, you can click on the “Random” button on the navigation bar. This will allow you to look at random anime within certain criteria that you choose. If you are feeling risky, you can go in without entering any specific settings and see what you get!
